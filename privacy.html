Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Whats Good LLC (operating the “What’s Good” app) (“we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and shares information when you use the What’s Good mobile application and related services (the “App”).

By using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

1Who we are

What’s Good is a map-first social app for discovering, sharing, and rating places and experiences. The App is operated by Whats Good LLC, a limited liability company organized in California, USA.

Contact: admin@whatsgoodapp.co

2Information we collect

a. Information you provide

  • Account information: email address and password (used to create and secure your account), and a username.
  • Profile information: display name, optional bio, profile photo, cover photo, and chosen interests/categories.
  • Content you create (“User Content”): photos and videos, captions, ratings and scores, category-specific rating criteria, place/business names you add, dishes/items, comments, and stories.
  • Social activity: the accounts you follow, likes, saved spots, collections, and direct messages you send.
  • Reports and blocks: if you report or block another user or piece of content.

b. Location information

  • With your permission, we collect your device’s precise location to place your posts on the map, power nearby/“Trending” discovery, calculate travel times and directions, and suggest the name of the place you’re at when you post.
  • You can enable or disable location access at any time in your device settings. Some features (map, nearby discovery, directions) will be limited without it.

c. Information collected automatically

  • Basic usage and device information needed to operate the App (for example, app version and general diagnostic information).
  • On-device preferences stored locally on your device (such as sound settings, search distance, recent searches, drafts, saved spots, and collections). This data stays on your device unless you post it.

We do not currently use third-party advertising networks or advertising trackers.

3How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage your account and profile;
  • Display your posts, ratings, and activity to you and (for public content) to other users;
  • Power map, search, “Trending,” leaderboards, directions, and personalized recommendations based on your interests;
  • Enable social features (following, comments, likes, messages, badges, and rankings);
  • Keep the App safe (reports, blocking, and moderation);
  • Respond to your requests and provide support;
  • Comply with legal obligations.

4How your information is shared

  • With other users: Content you mark public (posts, ratings, place pages, profile) is visible to other users. Private posts are visible only to you. Direct messages are visible to you and the recipient. Your profile, username, and public activity are visible to others.
  • Service providers (processors): We use trusted third parties to run the App:

- Supabase — database, authentication, and file storage (hosts your account and content). See supabase.com/privacy.

- Mapbox — maps, place search, geocoding, and directions. When you use these features, location coordinates are sent to Mapbox. See mapbox.com/legal/privacy.

- Expo — the framework the App is built with, which may process limited diagnostic data. See expo.dev/privacy.

- Apple — app distribution and, if applicable, in-app purchases.

  • Legal reasons: We may disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of our users or others.
  • Business transfers: If we are involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

We do not sell your personal information.

5Your choices and rights

  • Edit your information: update your profile, username, and photos in the App at any time.
  • Delete your account: you can permanently delete your account and associated content from Settings → Delete account. Deletion removes your account, posts, ratings, comments, likes, and follows. Some information may remain in backups for a limited period and may persist where others have already saved or shared it.
  • Location: control location access in your device settings.
  • Blocking and reporting: block users or report content from within the App.
  • Access / correction / deletion requests: you may contact us at admin@whatsgoodapp.co to exercise privacy rights available to you.

Depending on where you live (for example, the EU/UK under GDPR, or California under the CCPA/CPRA), you may have additional rights, such as the right to access, correct, delete, or port your data, and to object to certain processing. We will honor applicable requests; contact us at admin@whatsgoodapp.co.

6Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the App. When you delete your account, we delete or de-identify your personal information within a reasonable period, except where we must retain it to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, or enforce our agreements.

7Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encryption in transit and access controls (row-level security) on our database. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8Children’s privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us at admin@whatsgoodapp.co and we will delete it.

9International users

We are based in the United States, and your information may be processed in the United States or other countries where we or our service providers operate. These countries may have different data-protection laws than your own. By using the App, you consent to this transfer.

10Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you within the App or by other reasonable means and update the “Last updated” date above.
